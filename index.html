The big idea

The one framework to remember

Nearly every question in marketing comes back to four decisions. Learn them properly and the rest of the subject has somewhere to hang.

P1

Product

What you offer — features, quality, brand, packaging and the benefit it delivers.

P2

Price

What it costs — list price, discounts, credit terms and what the customer thinks it is worth.

P3

Place

How it reaches the customer — channels, retailers, coverage and logistics.

P4

Promotion

How you tell people — advertising, sales promotion, personal selling and public relations.

Exam habit: when a case study confuses you, ask “which P is this decision about?” It usually unlocks the answer in seconds.
