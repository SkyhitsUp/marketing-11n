Project work

Five project ideas that actually work

Each one can be done with a survey, some photos and a short written report.

Project ideas

  1. Compare two brands' marketing mixes — pick two soft-drink or snack brands and compare all 4 Ps.
  2. Packaging and buying behaviour — survey 20 people on how packaging affects their choice of a local product.
  3. Student stationery habits — a small market research project with a questionnaire and simple charts.
  4. Track a social media campaign — follow one brand's posts for two weeks and analyse its promotion mix.
  5. Eco-friendly packaging awareness — measure how much buyers know and whether they will pay more.

A simple report structure

  • Cover page and acknowledgements.
  • Objective of the project.
  • Method — survey, interview or observation.
  • Findings with tables, charts or photos.
  • Conclusion and what you learned.
  • Questionnaire or source list in the annexure.
Note: project requirements differ from school to school. Check the marking scheme and submission format with your teacher before you start.

Study habits

Five ways to make revision stick

1

Learn the frameworks first

4 Ps, STP, PLC, AIDA. Everything else attaches to one of these.

2

Use real examples

One local shop and one big brand give you examples for almost any answer.

3

Answer in points

Definition, then 3–4 short points, then an example. Easy for the examiner to mark.

4

Practise case studies

Underline the decision in the case, then name the P or concept it belongs to.

5

Teach it back

Explain a topic to a classmate in two minutes. Gaps show up fast.
